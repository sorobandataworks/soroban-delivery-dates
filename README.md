# Soroban Delivery Dates for WooCommerce

Estimated delivery dates for WooCommerce stores, calculated from your business days, an official holiday calendar (UK bank holidays by division, or Japanese national holidays), your order cut-off time and preparation time. Dates are shown as a range with their basis — estimates, never guarantees.

- WordPress.org: https://wordpress.org/plugins/soroban-delivery-dates/
- The calculation runs in the visitor's browser at view time, so dates stay correct behind full-page caches and CDNs.
- No external calls, no personal data stored, no order data read or written.
